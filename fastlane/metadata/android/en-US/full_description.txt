LogSleuth is an Android logging toolkit with two modes.

Viewer app: a polished live logcat viewer with level coloring, filters
(level/tag/keyword/regex/exclusion), saved filter presets, per-app filtering
(via Shizuku or root), background recording sessions, crash & ANR detection with
notifications, a floating bookmark bubble, and export to txt/zip with device
info.

Embedded SDK: developers can integrate logsleuth-sdk into their own app to
record its logs, crashes and ANRs with zero permissions and zero network. End
users share a log zip in two taps.

Reading other apps' logs needs a grant. Shizuku needs no root (on Android 11+
it can be activated on-device via Wireless Debugging). Root works too, if the
device has Magisk, KernelSU, or APatch — it stays off until you turn it on.
Or grant once over ADB: adb shell pm grant io.github.shiaho777.logsleuth.app
android.permission.READ_LOGS. On Android 13 and later that grant only starts
logcat while LogSleuth is on screen; Shizuku and root keep working in the
background. The embedded SDK mode needs no grant at all.

Open source (Apache-2.0). No ads, no tracking. Network is used only to check
GitHub for updates.
